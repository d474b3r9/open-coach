# Training journal — template

> Append-only log read at the start of each session by the skill `coaching-rules`. **Always append at the bottom of each section, never rewrite past entries.** The history stays intact even when a decision is revised — append a new entry that supersedes the old one. **Do not commit this file** — it lives in the gitignored `plans/` directory.

## Entry format

- ISO date `YYYY-MM-DD` in bold
- Visual tag: 🏁 race / 📊 measurement / ✅ validated decision / ❌ rejected approach / 💡 insight / ⚠️ alert
- 1-3 lines max per entry
- **Always state the why** (the reason matters more than the what in the long run)

---

## § Sessions run

> Append after each significant session (quality, long run, race). Not needed for routine easy runs.

- **YYYY-MM-DD** — `<Session type>` (`<distance>` km / `<duration>`) — `<average pace>` / HR `<bpm>`. `<How it felt and highlights>`.

---

## § Coaching decisions

> Append for each structural decision: adding/removing a session, phase change, volume recalibration, choice of test, etc.

- **YYYY-MM-DD** ✅ `<One-line decision>`. **Why**: `<reason>`.

---

## § Athlete insights

> Append when a pattern emerges or is confirmed (preference, constraint, reproducible physiological behaviour).

- **YYYY-MM-DD** 💡 `<Insight in 1-2 lines>`. **Source**: `<observations that led to this insight>`.

---

## § Metric changes

> Append for each change of a reference metric (VDOT, max/resting HR, weight, FTP).

- **YYYY-MM-DD** 📊 `<Metric: old value → new value>`. **Source**: `<test or recalibration>`.

---

## § Races

> Append after each race (official or timed all-out test).

- **YYYY-MM-DD** 🏁 `<Race name>` — `<distance>` in `<HH:MM:SS>` (`<category rank>`). **Highlights**: `<pacing, nutrition, weather, feeling>`. **Lessons**: `<2-3 lines for the next build-up>`.

---

## § Alerts / signals to monitor

> Append when a physical or data signal crosses a vigilance threshold (resting HR +5, tendon pain, poor sleep > 3 nights).

- **YYYY-MM-DD** ⚠️ `<Signal>`. **Action taken**: `<decision going forward>`.
