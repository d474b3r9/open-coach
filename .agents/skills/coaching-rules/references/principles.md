# Universal training principles

Reference loaded by the `coaching-rules` skill. These rules hold for every endurance sport; each sport's rules guide (`rules-<sport>`, e.g. `rules-running`) adds its own zones, sessions and watch-workout conventions.

## 1. 80/20

80% of weekly volume at easy intensity (Z1-Z2). 20% at intensity (threshold, race pace, VO2max, speed — each sport names its zones). Strict ratio — most amateur endurance athletes do their easy sessions too hard, which disrupts recovery and sinks quality sessions.

## 2. CTL progression +5/week max

Chronic load (CTL) must not climb by more than 5 points per week. Beyond that: injury risk and chronic fatigue. Derived rule: weekly volume never increases by more than ~10% from one week to the next.

## 3. Recovery -25 % volume every 4 cycles

Every 4 weeks, a **recovery week**: volume -25%, lighter quality (e.g. moderate fartlek instead of intervals). Recovery is where adaptation happens. Skipping a recovery week → progress plateaus after 8-10 weeks max.
